我用自己的话讲一遍

1.How to deal with the tangent problem? We let the secant line approach the tangent line. 当割线PQ中，点Q趋近于点P时，割线趋近于切线 $lim_{Q \to P}$ secant line = tangent line 2.How to compute the tangent line? What we know is the point $P(x_0,y_0)$,and what we need is the slope of the tangent line. $m = lim_{\Delta x \to 0 \frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$ for example:facing the function $f(x)=\frac{1}{x}$,the $m$ (slope) is the derivative of f at point $P$ 3.The example function I learnt in the class one is $f(x)=\frac{1}{x}$,one is $f(x)=x^n$ 4.Limits and Continuity there are two limits,one is the right-hand limit,another is the left-hand limit. R:$lim_{x \to x_0^+}$ + It means that $x$ goes to $x_0$ and $x > x_0$ L:$lim_{x \to x_0^-}$ It means that $x$ goes to $x_0$ and $x < x_0$ the continuity: If $lim_{x \to x_0} f(x)=f(x_0)$,we say that $f(x)$ is continuous. three conditions:1. limit exists (from both L and R and L = R) 2. $f(x_0)$ is defined 3. they(the limit and $f(x_0)$) are equal 5.Jump discontinuity
